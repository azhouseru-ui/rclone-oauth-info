Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

YouTube Factory Rclone is a private OAuth integration used by its owner to connect the rclone desktop utility with their own Google Drive account.

Data access and use

With the owner's consent, the integration can list, view, download, create, update, and delete Google Drive files. The owner uses this access solely for personal file transfer, archiving and backup operations.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are kept in the owner's local rclone configuration and are not embedded in this public website. File content is processed on the owner's computer and Google Drive. The site does not intentionally collect personal information or run analytics. Personal Google Drive data is not sold or provided to advertisers.

Control and revocation

The owner can revoke the Google Drive permission at any time through their Google Account security settings or stop using the rclone connection.
